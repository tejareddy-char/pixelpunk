Fill this in and share it with your team **before** you talk to a single user. It stops research from turning into "let's just chat to some people".

## 1. Background

- **Project:** [Product or feature name]
- **Why now:** [What triggered this research? A drop in a metric, a new feature, a redesign, complaints?]
- **What we already know:** [Existing data, past research, support tickets, analytics]

## 2. Research goal

> One sentence. "We want to understand **[who]** so that we can decide **[what decision]**."

Example: *We want to understand how freelance designers send invoices so that we can decide whether to build invoicing into the app.*

## 3. Research questions

What do **we** need to learn? These are not the questions you ask users.

1. [e.g. What tools do people use today, and why?]
2. [e.g. Where do they get stuck or frustrated?]
3. [e.g. What would make them switch?]

## 4. Method

| Method | Why this method | Participants |
|---|---|---|
| [User interviews] | [To understand motivations and context] | [6–8] |
| [Usability test] | [To see where people get stuck in the prototype] | [5] |
| [Survey] | [To check how common a problem is] | [100+] |

## 5. Participants

- **Who:** [Role, behaviours, experience level]
- **Must have:** [e.g. sent at least 3 invoices in the last month]
- **Exclude:** [e.g. employees, competitors, people in our last study]
- **Recruiting from:** [Customer list, User Interviews, social media, friends of friends]
- **Incentive:** [e.g. $50 gift card for 45 minutes]

## 6. Timeline

| Step | Owner | Date |
|---|---|---|
| Plan approved | [Name] | [Date] |
| Recruiting | [Name] | [Date] |
| Sessions | [Name] | [Date] |
| Analysis | [Name] | [Date] |

## 7. Output

- [e.g. 1-page summary with top 5 insights and recommendations]
- [e.g. Updated persona and journey map]
- [e.g. Highlight reel of video clips]

## 8. Stakeholders

- **Decision maker:** [Name]
- **Must watch at least one session:** [Names]
