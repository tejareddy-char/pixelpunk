## Writing tasks

Good tasks are **realistic scenarios** that don't give away the answer.

- ❌ "Click the Settings button and change your password."
- ✅ "You think someone may know your password. Make your account secure again."

### Task template

> **Task [#]: [Short name]**
> Scenario: [The situation and goal, in the user's words]
> Success: [What "done" looks like]
> Starts on: [Screen]

### Example tasks

1. **Sign up:** "You've heard about [product] from a friend. Create an account and set it up for your work."
2. **Core action:** "You have a new client. Add them and send them your first [quote/file/message]."
3. **Find information:** "You want to know how much the Pro plan costs per year. Find out."
4. **Recover from a problem:** "You sent something to the wrong person by mistake. Fix it."

## Script

### Intro (3 min)

> Thanks for joining. Today you'll try a few tasks on [a prototype of] our product. We're testing the design, not you, so if something is confusing, that's our fault and exactly what we want to find.
>
> Please **think aloud**: tell me what you're looking at, what you're trying to do and what you expect to happen. I may not answer questions during the tasks because I want to see what you'd do on your own.
>
> Is it OK if I record the screen and audio?

### Tasks (20–30 min)

Read each task aloud, or share it in the chat. After each one, ask:

- On a scale of 1–7, how easy or difficult was that? (1 = very difficult, 7 = very easy)
- What did you expect to happen when you [action]?

If they're stuck for more than 2 minutes, note it as a fail and move on kindly.

### Debrief (5 min)

- What was your overall impression?
- What was the most confusing part?
- What did you like?
- If you could change one thing, what would it be?

## What to say when…

| Situation | Say |
|---|---|
| They ask "Is this right?" | "What would you expect?" or "What would you do if I weren't here?" |
| They go silent | "What are you thinking right now?" |
| They blame themselves | "You're doing great. Anything confusing is what we need to fix." |
| They ask what a button does | "What do you think it does?" |
