A good persona summarises **real research**. If you haven't talked to users yet, call it a *proto-persona* and treat everything as an assumption to test.

## Tips

- **Skip age, hobbies and pet names** unless they change design decisions.
- **2–4 personas is plenty.** Pick one primary persona to design for first.
- **Use real quotes** from interviews. They make personas believable.
- **Revisit every 6–12 months**, or after new research.
- **Make them visible.** Pin them in your Figma file or team space and refer to them by name in design reviews.
